Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

1. Who runs this site

This personal website is run by an individual ("I", "me") in Germany. I decide why and how I process the data described here. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@bananabrother77.online.

2. What is collected

When Data
Any request Your IP address and normal request headers reach the CDN and server so they can return the page and protect the service.
Google Analytics Page path and query string, referrer, browser and device type, approximate location from IP, and cookies (_ga, _ga_G-TJCCV8RPV4, _gid).
Feedback form Category, rating, feedback text, optional contact info (email, Discord username, etc.), Cloudflare Turnstile token, user agent, and timestamp. Submissions are delivered to me through Discord via a webhook. Your IP is used for rate limiting and Turnstile verification but is not forwarded to Discord.
Theme sync (optional) When you enable "Sync theme across sites" in settings, a theme cookie is set so your preference is shared across all BananaBrother77 sites. A syncTheme cookie tracks whether sync is enabled. Never set without your explicit action.
Settings (localStorage) Theme preference, language choice, and sync setting stored in your browser's localStorage. Not sent to me unless you enable theme sync.
External content Pages load icons (Lucide via unpkg), fonts (hosted locally via jsDelivr), and images (GitHub raw) from CDNs. These services receive standard request data.

3. Why it is used

  • to deliver pages and features you request
  • to measure traffic and understand which parts of the site are used
  • to detect abuse and enforce rate limits
  • to handle feedback, support, and security reports
  • to sync your theme preference across sites (only after you enable it)

I do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Legal basis where the GDPR applies

Service security, feedback handling, and operation of requested features rely on legitimate interests under GDPR Art. 6(1)(f). Google Analytics relies on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25 TTDSG), which you give by accepting the cookie banner and can withdraw or change at any time in the settings menu of any BananaBrother77 website. The optional theme-sync cookies also rely on your consent, which you give by enabling the setting and can withdraw by disabling it. Cloudflare Turnstile loads only on the feedback page and relies on its own legitimate interest in bot detection.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Cookie Purpose Duration Set by
_ga, _ga_G-TJCCV8RPV4, _gid Google Analytics tracking Up to 2 years Google Analytics
theme Cross-site theme sync (optional) 1 year Me, only when you enable it
syncTheme Sync preference (optional) 1 year Me, only when you enable it

localStorage stores your theme, language, and sync preference. These are not cookies and are not automatically sent to me.

6. Who receives data

Recipient Role
Cloudflare Provides CDN, DNS, DDoS protection, and Turnstile CAPTCHA. Receives request data including your IP.
Google Analytics Provides page view analytics. Receives page visits, device info, approximate location, and sets cookies.
Discord Receives feedback content, contact details, and metadata when you submit the feedback form. Your IP is not forwarded to Discord.
jsDelivr / unpkg / GitHub External content hosts. Receive standard request data when loading resources.

7. Retention

Feedback submissions are kept in Discord while needed to handle the conversation or investigate abuse. You can ask me to delete a feedback entry. I will remove it from Discord. Everything else, including Google Analytics records, Cloudflare logs, and CDN logs, is not stored on my server and is not under my control. Their retention is governed by those services' own policies.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or delete your feedback entry by emailing support@bananabrother77.online. I can remove it from Discord, but I cannot delete data stored by third parties (Google Analytics, Cloudflare, CDNs). Those services have their own policies and you would need to contact them or use their opt-out tools.

You can disable Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or change your analytics choice at any time in the settings menu of any BananaBrother77 website. You can disable the theme-sync cookie at any time by unchecking the setting in the site's settings modal. You can clear localStorage and cookies through your browser settings at any time.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may change with the site. The date at the top will change when it does.

Privacy questions and requests: support@bananabrother77.online.
